# 主題5：健身運動 — Fitness & Health

## 核心詞彙語庫

### 健身習慣
| 英文 | 中文說明 |
|------|---------|
| I've been hitting the gym. | 我最近在健身 |
| I'm trying to stay consistent. | 我在努力保持規律 |
| I work out three times a week. | 我一週練三次 |
| I've been on and off lately. | 我最近有一搭沒一搭的 |
| I skipped leg day again. | 我又跳過腿日了 |

### 運動類型
| 英文 | 中文說明 |
|------|---------|
| Strength training | 重量訓練 |
| Cardio | 有氧運動 |
| HIIT (High-Intensity Interval Training) | 高強度間歇訓練 |
| Yoga / Pilates | 瑜伽 / 皮拉提斯 |
| Running / Cycling | 跑步 / 騎車 |

### 描述身體狀態
| 英文 | 中文說明 |
|------|---------|
| I'm sore all over. | 我全身酸痛 |
| I pulled a muscle. | 我拉傷了 |
| I'm out of breath. | 我喘不過氣 |
| I'm finally seeing results. | 我終於看到成效了 |
| I hit a plateau. | 我遇到瓶頸了（體重/體能停滯） |

### 飲食與健康
| 英文 | 中文說明 |
|------|---------|
| I'm watching what I eat. | 我在注意飲食 |
| I'm cutting back on carbs. | 我在減少碳水 |
| I'm trying to eat clean. | 我在吃乾淨飲食 |
| I've been drinking more water. | 我最近多喝水 |
| Cheat day | 作弊日（飲食放鬆的一天） |

## Turn Dialog 範例

### 4-Turn（健身聊天）
```
A: You look like you've been working out more lately.
B: Ha, yeah. I've been going to the gym about four times a week.
A: That's impressive. How do you stay motivated?
B: Honestly, I just remember how I feel after. That's enough to keep me going.
```

### 6-Turn（運動受傷）
```
A: You're limping — what happened?
B: I overdid it at the gym yesterday. Pretty sure I pulled something in my hamstring.
A: Oh ouch. Did you skip your warm-up?
B: Kind of. I was in a rush and just jumped into the weights.
A: Classic. Rest it for a few days and don't push through the pain.
B: Yeah, learned that the hard way. My ego was bigger than my common sense.
```
