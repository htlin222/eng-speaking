# 主題2：解釋立場 — Explaining Your Position

## 核心詞彙語庫

### 表達個人立場
| 英文 | 中文說明 |
|------|---------|
| From my perspective... | 從我的角度來看… |
| The way I see it... | 依我之見… |
| I strongly believe that... | 我堅信… |
| My take on this is... | 我對這件事的看法是… |
| If you ask me... | 如果你問我的話… |

### 澄清誤解
| 英文 | 中文說明 |
|------|---------|
| That's not what I meant. | 我不是那個意思 |
| Let me clarify. | 讓我說清楚一點 |
| What I'm trying to say is... | 我想說的是… |
| I think there's been a misunderstanding. | 我覺得有些誤會 |
| To be more precise... | 更精確地說… |

### 給出理由
| 英文 | 中文說明 |
|------|---------|
| The reason I say this is... | 我這樣說的原因是… |
| Based on my experience... | 根據我的經驗… |
| The evidence suggests... | 證據顯示… |
| Think about it this way... | 這樣想想看… |
| Here's the thing... | 重點是這樣… |

### 保持立場但接受異見
| 英文 | 中文說明 |
|------|---------|
| I respect your view, but... | 我尊重你的看法，但… |
| I see where you're coming from. | 我了解你的出發點 |
| We'll have to agree to disagree. | 我們就各持己見吧 |
| That's a fair point. | 這是個好觀點 |
| I hadn't thought of it that way. | 我沒從這個角度想過 |

---

## Turn Dialog 範例

### 4-Turn（表達決定）
```
A: Why did you decide to study abroad?
B: It's not that I didn't like it here — I just felt I needed a new challenge.
A: Makes sense. Were you scared?
B: Terrified, honestly. But I knew I'd regret it if I didn't try.
```

### 6-Turn（應對質疑）
```
A: Don't you think quitting without another job lined up is risky?
B: I understand the concern. But staying somewhere that's draining you has its own risks.
A: Like what?
B: Burnout, losing your passion, settling. Those costs are just harder to see.
A: I guess I never thought about it that way.
B: The visible risk always feels bigger than the invisible one.
```
