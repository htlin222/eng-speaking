# 主題8：人生深度談話 — Life & Philosophy

## 核心詞彙語庫

### 人生觀與價值觀
| 英文 | 中文說明 |
|------|---------|
| What matters most to me is... | 對我最重要的是… |
| I've come to realize that... | 我漸漸意識到… |
| Looking back, I think... | 回頭看，我覺得… |
| Life's too short to... | 人生太短暫，不應該… |
| I believe everything happens for a reason. | 我相信凡事皆有因 |

### 談論夢想與目標
| 英文 | 中文說明 |
|------|---------|
| My long-term goal is to... | 我的長期目標是… |
| I've always dreamed of... | 我一直夢想著… |

| It's never too late to start. | 開始永遠不嫌晚 |

### 談論挑戰與成長
| 英文 | 中文說明 |
|------|---------|
| That experience changed me. | 那段經歷改變了我 |
| I came out stronger on the other side. | 我從中走出來，變得更強 |
| Failure taught me more than success did. | 失敗教我的比成功更多 |
| I had to hit rock bottom first. | 我得先跌到谷底 |
| Growth is uncomfortable. | 成長是令人不舒服的 |

### 談論幸福與意義
| 英文 | 中文說明 |
|------|---------|
| I find meaning in... | 我在…中找到意義 |
| Happiness isn't a destination. | 快樂不是終點 |
| It's the little things that add up. | 是那些小事的累積 |
| I used to chase success. Now I chase fulfillment. | 我以前追求成功，現在追求滿足感 |
| At the end of the day... | 說到底… |

## Turn Dialog 範例

### 6-Turn（人生選擇）
```
A: If you could go back and change one decision, what would it be?
B: Honestly? I'd start something on my own sooner. I waited too long.
A: What held you back?
B: Fear of failure, mostly. And this idea that I needed to be "ready."
A: Did you ever feel ready?
B: No. I just realized waiting for readiness is just another word for waiting forever.
```

### 8-Turn（關於成功的定義）
```
A: Do you think you're successful?
B: That's a loaded question. Depends on how you define it.
A: How do you define it?
B: For me, it's waking up and genuinely wanting to do what I do. Not dreading Monday.
A: That's a good measure. Most people I know dread Monday.
B: Yeah. I think we've been sold this idea that success means a big salary and a fancy title.
A: And you don't buy that?
B: I used to. But I've met too many people with all that who are absolutely miserable.
```
