# 主題4：旅遊對話 — Travel

## 核心詞彙語庫

### 機場與交通
| 英文 | 中文說明 |
|------|---------|
| Which terminal is it? | 是哪個航廈？ |
| Is this the right platform? | 這是正確的月台嗎？ |
| How long does it take to get there? | 到那裡要多久？ |
| I missed my connection. | 我錯過轉機了 |
| Could you direct me to...? | 可以指引我去…嗎？ |

### 訂房與入住
| 英文 | 中文說明 |
|------|---------|
| I have a reservation under... | 我有預訂，名字是… |
| Is breakfast included? | 有包含早餐嗎？ |
| Could I get a room with a view? | 可以要一間有景觀的房間嗎？ |
| What time is check-out? | 退房時間是幾點？ |
| Could I store my luggage? | 可以幫我寄放行李嗎？ |

### 餐廳點餐
| 英文 | 中文說明 |
|------|---------|
| I'll have the... | 我要點… |
| Could I substitute...for...? | 可以把…換成…嗎？ |
| I'm allergic to... | 我對…過敏 |
| Could we get the bill, please? | 可以給我們帳單嗎？ |
| Is the tip included? | 有包含小費嗎？ |

### 問路與指引
| 英文 | 中文說明 |
|------|---------|
| Excuse me, do you know where...is? | 打擾一下，請問…在哪裡？ |
| Is it within walking distance? | 走路可以到嗎？ |
| You can't miss it. | 你不會找不到的 |
| Take the second left. | 走第二個路口左轉 |
| It's about a 10-minute walk. | 走路大約10分鐘 |

### 緊急狀況
| 英文 | 中文說明 |
|------|---------|
| I lost my passport. | 我的護照掉了 |
| I need to contact the embassy. | 我需要聯繫大使館 |
| Is there a pharmacy nearby? | 附近有藥局嗎？ |
| I need to see a doctor. | 我需要看醫生 |
| Can you help me? | 你能幫我嗎？ |

## Turn Dialog 範例

### 4-Turn（問路）
```
A: Excuse me, is the train station far from here?
B: Not at all. It's about a five-minute walk. Just go straight and turn left at the lights.
A: Got it. And is there a subway exit closer to the city center?
B: Exit 3 should put you right in the heart of it. You can't miss it.
```

### 6-Turn（機場緊急）
```
A: Excuse me, I think I missed my flight. What should I do?
B: Oh no. Do you have your boarding pass?
A: Yes, here. The gate closed five minutes ago.
B: Let me check with my supervisor. Sometimes they can still get you on if the plane hasn't pushed back.
A: Thank you so much. I really need to make this connection.
B: I understand. Hang tight — I'll be right back.
```
