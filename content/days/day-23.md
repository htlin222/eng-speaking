# Day 23 — Improvisation

## Practice Prompts

Respond to each without pausing more than two seconds. No preparation.

1. Describe what you can see right now as if you've never seen any of it before.
2. What's one thing about yourself that surprises people?
3. Tell me about a small decision that turned out to matter more than you expected.
4. What does a good day actually feel like for you?
5. Explain something you do well to someone who's never seen it done.
6. Describe a place that means something to you.
7. What's a question you've never been asked but wish someone would?
8. Talk about something you changed your mind about.
9. What do you think most people get wrong about you?
10. Tell me about a moment where you felt completely present.

---

## Sentences for When You're Caught Off Guard

- That's not something I've been asked before — let me think.
- The first thing that comes to mind is—
- I don't have a polished answer for that. Here's the rough version.
- That's a more complicated question than it looks.
- I'm going to answer this honestly rather than well.
- Instinctively, I'd say — though I'd want to think more before committing.

---

## Note

Improvisation is not about being brilliant under pressure. It's about staying **engaged and honest** when you don't have time to prepare. The goal is to keep speaking, not to be impressive.
