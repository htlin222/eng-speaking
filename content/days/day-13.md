# Day 13 — Agreeing & Disagreeing

## Sentences

- You took the words right out of my mouth.
- I was coming to the same conclusion, actually.
- That tracks with what I've been thinking.
- I'd agree up to a point, but not entirely.
- I see where you're coming from, even if I land somewhere different.
- I think we're talking about different things and calling them the same.
- That's a reasonable position — it's just not mine.
- I can accept that. I don't have to agree to move forward.
- We'll have to hold that one open for now.
- I respect the view. I just can't share it.

## Dialog

> A: So you don't think it was the right call?
> B: Not entirely. The instinct was right, but the timing was off.
> A: I'd argue the timing was the least of it.
> B: Okay — what mattered more, in your view?
> A: The communication. No one knew what was happening.
> B: Fair. That I can concede.

## Note

**"That tracks"** = that makes sense / aligns with what I know. More casual than "I agree" and suggests you're actively thinking it through, not just nodding along.
