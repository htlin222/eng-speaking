# Day 13 — Agreeing & Disagreeing

## Sentences

- I find myself in substantial agreement with the broad argument, though the specific case you've made gives me pause.
- I'd want to offer a modest challenge to the premise before I could endorse the conclusion.
- There's a version of what you're saying I can get behind entirely. This particular version, I'm less sure.
- I take your point, and I think it's a serious one. I just arrive at a different place when I follow it through.
- We may be in violent agreement about the destination and simply disagreeing about the road.
- I'm not sure this is a binary question, and I'd be reluctant to treat it as one.
- I think we're actually much closer than this conversation might suggest. The disagreement is narrower than it appears.
- I respect the position. I can't share it, but I understand what would make it compelling.
- That's a fair reading of the evidence. I just weight certain parts of it differently.
- I'd rather leave this genuinely open than arrive at false consensus. It serves neither of us.


## Dialog

> A: So you don't think it was the right call?
> B: Not entirely. The instinct was right, but the timing was off.
> A: I'd argue the timing was the least of it.
> B: Okay — what mattered more, in your view?
> A: The communication. No one knew what was happening.
> B: Fair. That I can concede.

## Note

**"That tracks"** = that makes sense / aligns with what I know. More casual than "I agree" and suggests you're actively thinking it through, not just nodding along.
