# 第三週：深度表達訓練

> 目標：從「能說」到「說得好」，學會引導話題、延伸對話

---

## Day 13 — 人生觀與價值觀

**Turn Dialog**：6-Turn

```
A: Do you think money equals success?
B: Not necessarily. I think success means different things to different people.
A: Like what, for you?
B: For me, it's more about having time for the things I care about.
A: That's a healthy way to look at it.
B: What about you? What does success look like for you?
```

**重點**：
- 把話題拋回給對方 (`What about you?`)
- 表達「因人而異」：`means different things to different people`

---

## Day 14 — 文化差異深聊

**Turn Dialog**：8-Turn

```
A: I noticed people here tend to be pretty indirect. Is that a cultural thing?
B: Yeah, I think so. We tend to avoid direct confrontation.
A: Interesting. Where I'm from, people are much more upfront.
B: Really? Does that ever cause misunderstandings?
A: Sometimes. But usually it clears things up faster.
B: I can see that. I guess both have pros and cons.
A: Exactly. There's no one-size-fits-all approach.
B: True. Adapting to different styles is its own skill.
```

**重點**：
- `Upfront` — 直接坦率
- `No one-size-fits-all` — 沒有放諸四海皆準的方法

## Day 15 — 深化聆聽回應技巧

**今日無對話文本** — 純訓練日

**訓練內容**：
1. 聽 5 段不同口音的英文（美式、英式、澳式）
2. 對每段內容做 30 秒即興口頭摘要
3. 錄音並回聽

**口音差異重點**：

| 說法 | 美式 | 英式 | 澳式 |
|------|------|------|------|
| 下午 | afternoon | afternoon | arvo |
| 加油站 | gas station | petrol station | servo |
| 很酷 | cool / awesome | brilliant | ripper |

## Day 16 — 討論時事

**Turn Dialog**：8-Turn

```
A: Have you been following the news lately?
B: Not really. It's been a bit overwhelming.
A: I get that. Sometimes it's better to step back.
B: Yeah, but then I feel out of the loop.
A: I know what you mean. It's hard to find a balance.
B: Do you have any go-to sources you trust?
A: A few podcasts, mostly. I find audio easier to handle than reading headlines.
B: That's actually a good idea. Any recommendations?
```

**重點**：
- `Out of the loop` — 消息落後
- `Go-to` — 首選的、慣用的

---

## Day 17 — 說故事技巧

**今日重點**：用「故事弧」說出一件事

**公式**：Setting → Problem → Turning Point → Resolution → Reflection

**範例口說練習**：

> "So last week I had this really awkward moment at work... 
> My boss asked me to present in front of the whole team, 
> and I completely blanked out in the middle. 
> But then I just took a deep breath and said, 
> 'Give me a second — I want to make sure I get this right.' 
> Everyone laughed and the tension broke. 
> I think that was the moment I realized confidence isn't about being perfect."

**訓練任務**：
- [ ] 用這個公式講一件自己的真實經歷（2 分鐘內）
- [ ] 錄音，評估：是否一氣呵成？節奏是否自然？

## Day 18 — 第三週回顧

**8-Turn 挑戰**：選一個主題，完成不中斷的 8 輪完整對話

**主題選項**：
- 「什麼叫做成功的人生？」
- 「你對遠端工作的看法是？」
- 「你會想移居國外嗎？為什麼？」
