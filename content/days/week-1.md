# 第一週：建立語感基礎

> 目標：打破中文思考習慣，讓耳朵先熟悉英文節奏

---

## Day 1 — 語感啟動

**主題**：自我介紹與日常問候  
**Turn Dialog**：2-Turn

```
A: Hey, how've you been?
B: Not bad, keeping busy. You?
A: Same here. Just got back from a trip.
```

**今日重點句型**：
- `How've you been?`（母語人士慣用縮寫，比 How have you been 更自然）
- `Keeping busy` — 比 "I'm busy" 更成熟的說法

**訓練任務**：
- [ ] 聽老師示範 3 遍
- [ ] 跟讀模仿語調
- [ ] 錄音存檔（這是你的 Day 1 基準音檔）

---

## Day 2 — 情感表達

**主題**：表達心情與感受  
**Turn Dialog**：2-Turn

```
A: You look tired today.
B: I don't feel like doing anything today, honestly.
A: That's fair. Take it easy.
```

**今日重點句型**：
- `I don't feel like doing anything` — 比 "I don't want to" 更成熟自然
- `That's fair` — 表示理解對方，萬用回應

**訓練任務**：
- [ ] 盲聽模式（關閉字幕）練習
- [ ] 跟讀 + 錄音

---

## Day 3 — 日常對話節奏

**主題**：聊天節奏與回應技巧  
**Turn Dialog**：4-Turn

```
A: Did you do anything fun this weekend?
B: Not really. I just stayed home and watched TV.
A: Yeah? What did you watch?
B: Some documentary on Netflix. It was actually really good.
```

**重點句型**：
- `Not really` — 軟性否定，比 "No" 更自然
- `actually` — 語氣轉折的萬用副詞

---

## Day 4 — 表達意見

**主題**：給出意見與看法  
**Turn Dialog**：4-Turn

```
A: What do you think about working from home?
B: Honestly, I think it depends on the person.
A: That's true. Some people need the office environment.
B: Exactly. I personally prefer a mix of both.
```

**重點句型**：
- `Honestly, I think...` — 自然表達個人觀點的開場
- `It depends on...` — 高頻萬用句

---

## Day 5 — 文化差異對話

**主題**：談論不同文化習慣  
**Turn Dialog**：4-Turn

```
A: Is it true that people in Taiwan work a lot of overtime?
B: Yeah, it's pretty common here. The work culture is quite intense.
A: That's different from what I'm used to. We usually leave right at 5.
B: I think it's slowly changing, but old habits die hard.
```

**重點句型**：
- `Old habits die hard` — 積習難改，地道慣用語
- `pretty common` — 比 "very common" 更口語
