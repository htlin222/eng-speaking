# Day 19 — Goals & Ambitions

## Sentences

- I want to be working toward something I can explain to myself.
- I keep setting the same goal and stopping at the same point.
- I've started thinking in decades rather than years.
- The goal keeps shifting, which I've learned is okay.
- I don't need it to be big — I need it to be mine.
- I'm more interested in the direction than the destination.
- I want to look back and feel like I moved forward deliberately.
- I've been too comfortable, which is its own kind of trap.
- The ambition was always there. I just didn't think I was allowed to have it.
- I'd rather try and be wrong than not try and always wonder.

## Dialog

> A: Where do you see yourself in five years? And I mean genuinely, not the interview answer.
> B: Doing something I built. Doesn't have to be big — just mine.
> A: That's more honest than most people's answers.
> B: Most people give the answer they think they should have. I've stopped doing that.
> A: What changed?
> B: I got tired of performing ambition I didn't actually feel.

## Note

**"Direction rather than destination"** acknowledges that goals evolve. It's a frame for people who have drive but haven't yet found a fixed endpoint — and that's most people.
