# 第二週：情境對話展開

> 目標：將句型套入真實生活情境，從被動模仿到主動輸出

---

## Day 7 — 旅遊對話

**主題**：問路、訂房、機場溝通  
**Turn Dialog**：4-Turn

```
A: Excuse me, is this the right platform for the airport?
B: I think so, but you might want to double-check with the staff.
A: Good idea. Thanks for the heads up.
B: No problem. Safe travels!
```

**重點句型**：
- `Heads up` — 事先提醒，非常口語
- `Safe travels` — 道別時的地道說法

---

## Day 8 — 健身運動

**主題**：健身房、運動習慣、健康話題  
**Turn Dialog**：4-Turn

```
A: Have you been going to the gym lately?
B: On and off. I've been trying to stay consistent.
A: Same. It's hard to keep up with a routine.
B: Yeah, but once you get into the habit, it gets easier.
```

**重點句型**：
- `On and off` — 三天打魚兩天曬網
- `Keep up with` — 跟上、維持

---

## Day 9 — 安慰朋友

**主題**：當朋友情緒低落時如何回應  
**Turn Dialog**：6-Turn

```
A: I've been feeling really down lately.
B: I'm sorry to hear that. What's going on?
A: Work has just been overwhelming. I can't seem to keep up.
B: That sounds really tough. Have you talked to anyone about it?
A: Not really. I guess I've just been keeping it to myself.
B: Well, I'm here if you need to talk. You don't have to go through this alone.
```

**重點句型**：
- `Feeling down` — 情緒低落
- `Overwhelming` — 喘不過氣
- `You don't have to go through this alone` — 安慰對方的核心句

---

## Day 10 — 解釋立場

**主題**：說明自己的想法或決定  
**Turn Dialog**：6-Turn

```
A: Why did you decide to quit your job?
B: It's not that I hated it — I just felt like I wasn't growing anymore.
A: That makes sense. Did you have something lined up?
B: Not exactly. I'm taking some time to figure things out.
A: That takes guts. Most people would just stick it out.
B: Maybe. But life's too short to stay somewhere that drains you.
```

**重點句型**：
- `It's not that...` — 澄清立場的萬用開場
- `Have something lined up` — 有後續計畫
- `Takes guts` — 需要勇氣

---

## Day 11 — 職場溝通

**主題**：會議、同事互動、職場禮貌英文  
**Turn Dialog**：6-Turn

```
A: Did you get a chance to look at the report I sent?
B: I glanced at it, but I haven't had time to go through it properly.
A: No worries. Let me walk you through the key points.
B: That would be helpful, actually.
A: Basically, the main takeaway is that we need to adjust our timeline.
B: Okay. Let's circle back after I've had a chance to digest it.
```

**重點句型**：
- `Get a chance to` — 有機會做某事
- `Walk you through` — 帶你了解、說明
- `Circle back` — 稍後再跟進
