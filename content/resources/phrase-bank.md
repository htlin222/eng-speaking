# 600句萬用句庫

> 依主題分類，每類 60-70 句，聚焦高頻語料。掌握這 20%，解鎖 80% 日常對話。

---

## 一、打招呼與開場（60句）

### 問候
- How've you been? / How are you doing?
- What's new with you? / What have you been up to?
- Long time no see! You look great!
- It's so good to see you again.
- How's everything going?

### 開啟話題
- Hey, I wanted to ask you something.
- Have you heard about...?
- Speaking of which...
- That reminds me...
- Oh, by the way...

### 結束對話
- It was great catching up.
- We should do this more often.
- I'll let you go. Talk soon!
- Stay in touch, okay?
- Take care of yourself.

---

## 二、表達意見（70句）

### 同意
- Exactly. / Totally. / Absolutely.
- You took the words right out of my mouth.
- I couldn't agree more.
- That's exactly how I feel.
- You make a really good point.

### 不同意（禮貌）
- I see your point, but...
- I'm not sure I agree with that.
- I'd look at it differently.
- Have you considered...?
- That's one way to look at it.

### 表達個人觀點
- Honestly speaking...
- From where I stand...
- The way I see it...
- My take on this is...
- If you ask me...

### 不確定
- I'm not entirely sure, but...
- It's hard to say.
- It could go either way.
- I'm on the fence about it.
- I'd need to think about that more.

## 三、情感表達（60句）

### 正面情緒
- I'm over the moon!
- I couldn't be happier.
- That made my day.
- I'm so relieved.
- I feel like a weight's been lifted.

### 負面情緒
- I'm feeling a bit down.
- I'm exhausted / drained.
- I'm frustrated with...
- I'm overwhelmed.
- I'm at my wit's end.

### 安慰他人
- I'm sorry to hear that.
- That sounds really tough.
- I'm here for you.
- You're not alone in this.
- It's going to be okay.

## 四、工作與職場（70句）

### 會議
- Let's get started. / Shall we kick things off?
- Could you elaborate on that?
- I'd like to revisit that point.
- Let's take a five-minute break.
- Any final questions before we wrap up?

### 任務與跟進
- I'll take care of it.
- Leave it to me.
- I'll have it done by...
- Just following up on...
- Could you give me a status update?

### 建議
- What if we...?
- Have you thought about...?
- One option would be to...
- It might be worth trying...
- That could work if...

## 五、日常生活（70句）

### 食物
- I'm starving / I'm full / I'm stuffed.
- That was absolutely delicious.
- I've been craving...
- Let's grab a bite to eat.
- What do you feel like having?

### 娛樂
- Have you seen/watched/read...?
- I just finished binge-watching...
- I'm into... lately.
- It's totally worth watching.
- The ending blew my mind.

### 天氣
- It's gorgeous out today.
- What miserable weather.
- I can't stand this humidity.
- Looks like it might rain.
- Finally, some sunshine!

## 六、旅遊（60句）

### 問路
- Could you point me to...?
- Is it within walking distance?
- How long does it take to get there?
- Is there a shortcut?
- You can't miss it.

### 住宿
- I have a reservation under...
- Is breakfast included?
- Could we get an early check-in?
- Is there a gym/pool/spa?
- Could you recommend a nearby restaurant?

### 緊急
- I've lost my...
- Could you call for help?
- Is there a hospital nearby?
- I need to speak to someone in English.
- Please contact the embassy.

## 七、人際關係（60句）

### 讚美
- You're really good at this.
- I admire how you handle things.
- You always know the right thing to say.
- That was really thoughtful of you.
- You've come such a long way.

### 感謝
- I really appreciate it.
- I don't know what I'd do without you.
- You've been incredibly helpful.
- That means a lot to me.

### 道歉

- I should have handled that better.
- I take full responsibility.
- Let me make it up to you.
- That was completely my fault.

## 九、即興應答（50句）

### 爭取時間
- That's a great question.
- Let me think about that.
- How do I put this...
- Off the top of my head...
- Give me a second.

### 表達模糊

- It's hard to explain.
- Something along those lines.
- You know what I mean?
- Does that make sense?

### 轉換話題
- Anyway, the point is...
- But that's a whole other story.
- Long story short...
- To get back on track...
- Moving on...
